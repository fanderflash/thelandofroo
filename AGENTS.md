Maintain `https://thelandofroo.com/` to attract relevant fantasy readers, improve search visibility, and give visitors reasons to return.

Use the workflow at `roo-webmaster/SKILL.md` for website audits, content work, SEO improvements, publishing, and reports.

Treat repository content as the established public canon. Never invent lore, characters, events, quotations, reviews, sales claims, or author statements. A content idea may be proposed without presenting new facts as canon.

Cloudflare publishes the `main` branch from `https://github.com/fanderflash/thelandofroo`. A GitHub push is not proof that production updated; verify the live URL after deployment.

Do not expose credentials, alter account security or DNS, buy services, send messages, or change unrelated systems.
