Character · Arwen

Karden

Cunning, selfish, manipulative — and followed by a pattern of misfortune nobody can explain.

Karden is the third Arwen brother. He thinks primarily about his own advantage and can change position quickly when a different outcome looks better for him.

He is cunning and manipulative, with little interest in wider moral or political questions unless they affect him personally.

Objects break around him. Structures fail. Accidents happen. Whether Karden causes these events consciously, unconsciously, or is simply followed by a curse remains deliberately unresolved.
