Places of Roo

Hammerhill

Iron · timber · ancient stone

Hammerhill is a smithing settlement built across a dramatic green hill. Rooan timber halls and small workshops stand beside the remains of a far older Vanir structure — stonework made on a scale no human builder would choose.

At the heart of the settlement, a large one-storey hall has grown directly out from the ruin. Above it, pieces of the Vanir structure still rise over the hilltop.

A village built beside the old world
Human hands work in wood and iron. The stone was here long before them.

Human scale / Vanir scale

Two civilizations in one place.

The difference is visible immediately. The Rooans build in timber: long halls, huts, workshops, fences and narrow lanes. The Vanir remains are stone — carefully cut, monumental and built from blocks far larger than anything used in ordinary human construction.

The ruin was built for beings of a different scale. A single Vanir level can rise roughly as high as several human storeys. Even broken, the surviving architecture still makes the settlement around it feel temporary by comparison.

Hammerhill is smaller than Upperhall and surrounded by lush deciduous forest. Oaks and beeches grow larger here than their counterparts in our world, and the vegetation of Roo carries the same slightly heightened scale.
