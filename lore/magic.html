Six principles

The Magic of Roo

Truth, Lie, Dream, Silence, Ambiguity and the Unknown.

Every established form of magic in Roo is bound to one of six metaphysical principles. The strength of a magic user depends on discipline, life-force or spirit, and faith or conviction.

Truth magic strengthens or weakens what is already true or already exists. Lie magic allows something untrue or normally impossible to function as reality. Dream magic is associated with visions and perception beyond ordinary time. Silence is the magic of concealment and suppression. Ambiguity operates through uncertainty. Unknown magic is associated with the Aesir and has no known inherent limit.

Lie and Truth

A powerful magical lie requires extraordinary strength and conviction. It can collapse when successfully challenged, and it may weaken over long periods if even its caster begins to lose the ability to believe fully in it.

The witch later teaches Fander about Truth magic and how truth can challenge a magical lie.

The Unknown

Unknown magic changes the underlying nature or foundation of something. Unlike Dream magic, it does not provide the same ability to see the future.
