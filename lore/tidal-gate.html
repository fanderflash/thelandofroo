Ancient mechanism

The Tidal Gate

A sea gate left behind by a civilization humanity no longer understands.

The Tidal Gate is a vast ancient sea lock or gate built into an enormous wall-like structure between two seas.

Its scale and surviving mechanisms place it among the clearest reminders that the ancient builders possessed capabilities far beyond present humanity.

People in Roo can observe what the structure does without understanding the principles that make it possible. That gap between function and comprehension is typical of the ancient world.
