# The Land of Roo — visual canon

Use this reference whenever artwork is generated, selected, replaced, or cropped.

## Standard

- Finished imagery should look like premium cinematic dark-fantasy key art with realistic materials, natural atmosphere, and a restrained painterly photographic finish.
- Roo is northern, cold, fertile, weathered, and lived in. Use charcoal, slate blue, cold teal, pine green, weathered brown, muted bronze, and restrained amber light.
- Human buildings are timber, practical, and comparatively small. Vanir structures are ancient stone, monumental, and built on a scale that does not fit humanity.
- Ancient mechanisms may be visible to the audience, but medieval inhabitants do not understand them as modern machinery.
- Keep scenes specific to the subject of the page. Do not reuse a generic mountain scene across unrelated pages.
- Avoid flat vector art, plastic CGI, cartoon rendering, oversaturation, game-interface imagery, generic castles, excessive glowing runes, visible text, logos, and watermarks.
- Character appearance and world facts must follow the Canon Bible. Do not invent locked physical details when the source is silent.

## Web production

- Master composition: landscape 16:9.
- Website delivery size: 1600 × 900 WebP for normal hero and card use.
- Large source target: at least 1920 × 1080; retain the best available original outside the delivery path.
- Aim for roughly 150–500 KB per hero after compression.
- Compose important subjects toward the right or center-right when page titles appear on the left.
- Verify every image at desktop and mobile widths. Character faces must remain visible after cropping.
- Give informative images useful alt text. Decorative background images rely on the adjacent heading and text.
