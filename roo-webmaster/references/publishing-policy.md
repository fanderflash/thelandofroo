# Canon and publishing policy

Use this policy for every new page, meaningful copy change, quiz, timeline, glossary entry, caption, image prompt, or other public content.

## Sources and precedence

The private Google Drive document titled **The Land of Roo – Canon Bible** is the primary consistency guide. Locate it by exact title through the connected Google Drive account only when a content run needs it. The larger private document titled **The Land of Roo** is manuscript context, not a source that may be exposed or summarized publicly.

The Canon Bible is a guardrail, not blanket publication permission. Facts that appear only in a private source remain private until Thomas approves them for the site. Never quote, link to, expose, or reveal the private documents or their metadata on the public website.

For automatic publication, the current public website and public repository content define what is already public. When sources conflict, preserve the Canon Bible's locked names and spellings. Do not resolve any other story conflict publicly; stop and ask Thomas.

## Level 1 — may be published automatically

Only publish Level 1 content when `publish_enabled` is true and all claims can be traced to facts already public on the website. Allowed subjects are:

- the established magic system, lore, characters, and places;
- beginner and “start here” guides;
- established religion, society, peoples, cultures, history, and timelines;
- established creatures, mysteries, ruins, and ancient technology without revealing hidden explanations;
- glossaries, indexes, archive hubs, FAQs, reading paths, relationship overviews, maps, and quizzes derived entirely from public canon;
- internal links, navigation copy, summaries, metadata, accessibility text, and formatting improvements;
- factual project news explicitly supplied by Thomas for publication.

Level 1 work may reorganize, summarize, clarify, and connect public canon. It may not turn implication into fact, add story events, or answer an intentionally unresolved mystery.

## Level 2 — create a draft and obtain approval

Prepare a reviewable draft but do not publish:

- facts found in the private Canon Bible or manuscript that are not already public;
- any Open Canon addition, including new minor people, settlements, customs, legends, foods, creatures, ruins, or anecdotes;
- new artwork, maps that add geographic facts, substantial image changes, or a major visual redesign;
- new author commentary, thematic interpretation, inspiration claims, or project news not explicitly supplied for publication;
- content where spoiler status, canon status, sourcing, or the intended audience is uncertain.

The draft report must list every proposed new fact and identify its source category without copying private source passages.

## Level 3 — never publish automatically

Do not automatically publish future plot events, deaths, wars, relationships, discoveries, secret identities, hidden motivations, unresolved truths, or other spoilers. Do not publish new major characters, countries, religions, factions, magic rules, commercial claims, prices, release dates, legal terms, rights-policy changes, or personal statements in Thomas's voice.

Level 3 material requires a new explicit instruction from Thomas for the specific publication.

## Visual standard

Preserve the site's established cinematic northern-fantasy identity: near-black and deep green surfaces, restrained warm gold accents, serif typography, dramatic natural light, monumental landscapes, and the contrast between human timber and ancient inhuman stone. Favor atmosphere, clarity, and believable texture over decorative effects.

Do not introduce bright generic fantasy colors, game-interface styling, modern technology imagery, inconsistent character appearances, or visual details unsupported by public canon. Improve spacing, hierarchy, mobile navigation, readability, crops, consistency, and performance without approval when the change does not alter the site's identity. New generated artwork remains Level 2.

## Required publication check

Before publication, record which level applies, the public URLs or repository files supporting every factual claim, affected pages, desktop and mobile visual evidence, and test results. If any claim cannot be supported as Level 1, downgrade the work to a draft. After deployment, verify the public page and confirm that no private fact or source reference leaked.
